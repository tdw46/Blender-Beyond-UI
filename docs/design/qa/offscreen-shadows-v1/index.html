How it works

The light-space raster remains the fast candidate check. Uncertain rays still use the full geometric search. Four existing anti-aliasing samples now share node and triangle loads while retaining their individual rejection tests. Already-resolved rays stop participating.

Traversal metadata fits in the unused lanes of existing bounds textures. A small GPU consolidation pass publishes a compact lookup without increasing the existing 128 MiB query budget or adding geometry readback.

Quality and safety

  • 96 full-scene frames: zero changed pixels at 2.5, 8.81, 50 and 250 mm.
  • 162 synthetic alpha/material cases, plus 12 recorded-geometry replay cases.
  • All offscreen casters, samples, alpha cutouts, softness, sidedness and accepted CamKeys geometry retained.
  • Windows and older Blender paths unchanged. New fast path is gated to qualified 5.2 Metal.

Remaining limits

2.5 mm is still heavier than ordinary lenses. This is a substantial acceleration, not a 60fps claim. Full-size viewport testing after restart remains open.

The shader changes are installed on disk and load on the next Blender launch. The live user scene was neither saved nor restarted. No new GIF or broad visual acceptance is claimed.
