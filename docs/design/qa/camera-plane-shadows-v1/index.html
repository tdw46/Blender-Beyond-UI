Performance work still open

The isolated receiver-search replay improved by roughly 5–35%, with identical output across all 12 cases. Full-scene improvement is modest and does not resolve the 2.5 mm stall. A diagnostic shadow bypass measured approximately 18.2 ms; it was never installed. Cold rebuilds, source ordering, broader depth prepass admission and constant-alpha specialization did not solve the remaining case and were not shipped.

The installed BVT shader update requires a real Blender restart. The user's live scene was neither saved nor restarted. Physical Windows testing and exhaustive motion/material acceptance remain separate.
